Ownership

Once the decision is made, the journey continues.

Here’s what happens after you choose — one step at a time.

  1. Decision You choose your Heysea. Our team confirms the specification, timeline and every custom detail with you before anything is signed.
  2. Purchase Clear contracts, staged payments and a dedicated project manager — every milestone is documented and shared with you in real time.
  3. Delivery Sea trials, handover and crew familiarisation at the port of your choice. We stay on board until you feel completely at home.
  4. Aftercare Warranty, global service partners and seasonal check-ups keep your yacht ready for the next horizon — for as long as you own her.
